  • Overhead Space 400–500 sq ft for sleeping/office (10 ft ceiling)
  • Total Per Person 1,400–1,500 sq ft

  • Construction Soundproof, fireproof, bulletproof glass
  • Indoor Garden 20 ft ceiling for growing plants, herbs, and trees
  • Views Lush green gardens and bird sanctuaries

Family Units

  • Example: Family of 57,500 sq ft combined space

Food Production Innovation

Fruit City doesn't just feed its residents — it feeds the world, using a fraction of the land.

🌳
Tree Towers

Vertical Growing Structures

Similar in structure to cell phone towers, Tree Towers grow plants sideways and upside down — achieving yields that would be impossible with traditional horizontal farming.

  • 1,000–2,000 times more trees in the same footprint
  • 400–700% increased yield per tree
  • Solar-powered dehumidifiers for water-free irrigation
  • LED lighting systems for lower growing levels
🌿
Advanced Techniques

Nutrient Density & Organic Methods

Fruit City produce is cultivated with the most advanced organic methods known — restoring the nutritional content that industrial farming has stripped from the food supply.

  • Replanting technique producing 5x larger root systems = 5x larger trees
  • Most nutrient-dense organic fertilizers available
  • Produce 2–4 times more nutritious than grocery store equivalents
  • Waste-converted liquid fertilizer 6–10x more powerful than commercial products

Atmospheric Enhancement

One of Fruit City's most ambitious goals: rebuild the atmosphere to its prehistoric strength.

💨

Hyperbaric Air Conditions

Mimicking prehistoric atmospheric conditions — based on Dr. Carl Baugh's research — hyperbaric environments have been shown to extend insect lifespans 2–3x in the first generation, neutralize snake venom, and accelerate healing.

🌲

Rebuilding the Ozone

The ultimate goal of the Tree Tower program is to plant enough structures globally to rebuild the atmosphere to 10x its current strength — restoring conditions that once supported prehistoric life and far greater biodiversity.

🏥

Healing Properties

Hyperbaric tabernacles are planned as centers for refugee training, education, and healing — bringing the benefits of enriched-atmosphere environments to the populations who need them most.

Energy & Transportation

Zero pollution. Unlimited supply. A complete departure from fossil fuels.

⚡
Hydrogen Power

Converting All Engines to Hydrogen

Hydrogen is the most abundant and efficient element in the universe — and it burns completely clean. Fruit City's plan converts all gas engines to hydrogen fuel through industrial conversion plants.

  • Zero pollution (vs. 220 toxic chemicals released by petroleum combustion)
  • Industrial conversion plants for vehicle fleets
  • Underwater windmills generating electricity from hurricane and cyclone energy
