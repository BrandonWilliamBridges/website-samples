Privacy

What we keep about you, why, and how to ask us to delete it.

Home › Privacy

Fruit City Worldwide only keeps what you give us, and only to stay in touch with you about our work. This page says what that is, in plain words.

If you apply to volunteer

The form on our Volunteer page sends us:

  • your first and last name, email address and phone number;
  • the area you live in, when you can help, what you are interested in, and anything you tell us about your experience or schedule;
  • if you need court-ordered community service hours: that you need them, how many hours, and your deadline. We do not ask for case numbers, charges or court papers, so please do not send them.

Applications go into Fruit City’s private records system. Only our administrators and the few team members who review volunteer applications can see them. We use them to reply to you and to match you with an activity. Your name, email address, phone number and answers (but not your community service details) are also saved as a volunteer contact record in the same system, and one of our administrators gets an email saying a new application arrived.

To stop automated junk, the form keeps a scrambled code made from your network address for up to two days. The code can’t be turned back into the address, and it is never kept with your application.

If you sign up for our newsletter

The sign-up form sends us your email address, and, if you choose to give them, your first name, city, country and the topics you are interested in. If you leave the country blank, we note the country your connection comes from instead. We keep this, with the time you agreed to get our emails, in our records system so we can send you the newsletter and follow up, and our team gets a text message with your details telling them someone signed up.

People we work with

Fruit City Worldwide staff keep contact records for the people and organizations we work with, such as partners, volunteers, donors and places of worship: names, contact details, and notes about our conversations and follow-ups. These records are private to our staff.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • We do not use it for advertising.

Services we use

Our website, forms and records system run on Cloudflare. Text messages to our team are sent through Twilio. If you pick a language from the globe menu, your choice is remembered in your own browser. If you choose to read a page through Google Translate, Google receives that page.

Asking us to see, change or delete your information

Email us at info@fruitcityworldwide.org and tell us what you would like us to do. Please say which email address or phone number you used, so we can find your records. We will reply to let you know when it is done.
